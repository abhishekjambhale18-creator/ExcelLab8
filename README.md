### 📌 Project Overview
This lab is a comprehensive PivotTable masterclass. Multiple PivotTables answer critical business questions related to sales, profit, customers, cities, payment modes, and time trends.

### 🔍 Analysis Covered
- Region-wise Sales & Profit
- Category-wise Sales
- Monthly & Yearly Trends
- Payment Mode distribution
- City-wise performance
- Customer Type contribution
- Average Sales & Profit comparisons

### 📊 Quick Insights
- **North** is the top-performing region
- **Electronics** generates the highest sales
- Sales are well distributed across all 12 months
- Payment modes (UPI, Card, Cash) are almost equally popular

### 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated
- Advanced PivotTables
- Grouping by Month & Year
- Multiple value fields
- Calculated insights
- Business storytelling with PivotTables

### 🔍 How to Explore Interactively
1. Open the workbook
2. Click on any PivotTable
3. Use the PivotTable Fields pane to create custom views
4. Double-click any number to drill down into the underlying transactions
