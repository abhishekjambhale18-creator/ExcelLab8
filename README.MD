# 📊 Excel — Pivot Tables & Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Description

Performed sales data analysis and summarization in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables and the DMart Sales Dataset. The assigned tasks involved analyzing sales, profit, customer types, regions, categories, cities, and payment modes to generate meaningful business summaries.

## 🧩 Operations Performed

01 — Region-wise Sales Analysis Used Pivot Tables to summarize total sales across different regions.

02 — Category-wise Sales Analysis Analyzed sales across product categories to compare their contribution to total sales.

03 — Monthly & Yearly Sales Analysis Summarized sales by month and year to examine sales patterns over time.

04 — Payment Mode Analysis Analyzed transaction counts across different payment modes and regions.

05 — City & Customer Analysis Summarized sales across cities and customer types to understand their contribution to overall sales.

06 — Region-wise Profit Analysis Analyzed total profit generated across different regions using Pivot Tables.

07 — Average Sales & Profit Analysis Compared average sales by category and region, and average profit by city and product category.
